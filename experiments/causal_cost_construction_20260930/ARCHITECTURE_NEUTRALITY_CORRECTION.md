# Architecture-neutrality correction — 2026-09-30 15:31 UTC

## What was wrong and what changed

The first working note overstated the admission rule as reducing BOTH arithmetic
and information access by >=10x, and required a future reusable schedule to also
reduce arithmetic. Those are not universal requirements of the governing
Constructive Theory Contract. A credible paid >=10x route and full target-time
closure are required; each architecture must pay its actual arithmetic, movement,
preparation, state and auxiliary costs. Retained arithmetic may fit a valid
schedule and is not itself a rejection.

Corrected the objective, comparison, stationary-schedule disposition and blocker
in WORKING_NOTE.md. Its event method's two <=0.1 inequalities are now expressly a
candidate-specific simultaneous-reduction claim. In NATIVE_FIELD_WRAPPER.md,
beta<0.05 is now expressly conditional on choosing a tenfold coefficient-byte
goal; it is not a universal field-source admission threshold. The grid proof,
finite field bridge, counterexample, byte inventories and exactness scope are
unchanged.

## Does the correction open an executable route?

It restores a valid class to consideration: retain the exact arithmetic but
amortize cold weight reads across causally available vectors and execute a more
efficient batched native kernel. Arithmetic utilization may change elapsed time
without changing the scalar product count. Likewise, reducing arithmetic could
help a compute-bound architecture without a separate 90% traffic reduction.
Neither observation supplies a new causal source, native kernel equivalence,
resource plan or sufficient time bound in the present record.

For the explicit weight-stationary candidate, a conservative block-time upper
bound would sum actual bounded load, kernel, address, state, synchronization,
proposal and repair service costs for its finite schedule. Shared tile reads may
be counted once only when those vector operands are causally available. Replacing
that sum by an overlap bound needs a valid resource/stall schedule. A source read
count or advertised peak FLOPS/bandwidth is not such an upper bound.

If K trial vectors yield A committed outputs, merely dividing block time by A
does not prove the required per-token p50/p95 or TTFT. The frozen delivery/event
protocol and common workload population must establish the actual output times,
and the same-machine ratio needs a certified baseline lower bound or a directly
proved coupled comparison. None is supplied by this correction.

The concrete generation schedule still lacks its paid causal vector source and
its native-exact batched-kernel lift. No new machine service guarantees, physical
allocation or baseline measurements were obtained. For the single-vector full
scan, 803108552704 cold bytes is an inventory of that stated schedule, not a
latency impossibility theorem. For the numeric coded source, no actual layout
was constructed. Thus no candidate is newly admitted here, but its nonadmission
must be based on those missing constructive/time facts, not on retained arithmetic.

This correction removes an unjustified exclusion; it does not improve a lower
bound, establish a new upper bound or count as dominant-cost research progress.
No runtime/model/GPU tests, new hardware assumptions, root-ledger changes, commits
or publication are performed. The grid/field bridge remains conditional and
compact; full O1-O6 remain OPEN.
