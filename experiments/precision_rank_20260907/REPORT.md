## Three candidate mechanisms and decision
A: change the numeric algebra, construct a low-inner-dimension source modulo 2^K, and centered-lift the entire dense output. No sparse output or previous-input similarity assumption. Unlike the prior full-residue-plane source, attempt fewer linear channels. Paid entry route is 2nr<=n^2/10 plus all bitwidth/preparation/state costs. Evaluate r at sufficient output precision, not merely modulo2. The generic samples fail this gate; no larger backend.
B: allow nonlinear decoding of one dense bounded-vector integer message. Base-(2H+1) code/inverse is explicit, but one scalar needs ceil(m log2(2H+1)) bits. For m4,H3, all2401 vectors roundtrip in12bits; codes0 and64 collide modulo64. This is a code-only decoder counting statement, not an online-read lower bound with x/side information. No cheap producer was constructed.
C: singular-limit/border-rank bilinear producer. The variable-W matrix-vector tensor's W-flattening is a permutation matrix of rank mn (rows e_j tensor e_i). r bilinear terms give flatten rank<=r and these minor conditions are closed under limits, hence border rank>=mn. Verified m3,n4 rank12/minor absdet1. NOT a bound on arbitrary fixed-checkpoint preprocessing or nonlinear programs.

## Actual constructor and source-only query
Bounded domain: square n<=128; nonzero integer W coefficients abs<=255; integer input abs<=7. No implicit quantization; float inputs/weights, including float negative zero, rejected. All these integer values are exactly BF16.
B=7 max_i sum_j abs(W_ij); K=bit_length(2B); Q=2^K>2B. Dense local elimination chooses the minimum 2-adic valuation pivot; swaps rows/columns, scales by an odd inverse and clears pivot row/column, tracking inverse operations. At most n pivots. Actual L,D,R satisfy W=LDR modQ, D=diag(2^e_i or0). Constructor verifies reconstruction and independent mod2 invertibility of L,R. Preparation, dense transformations and verification are charged.
r_K counts e_i<K. Store U=L[:,:r]D_nonzero, V=R[:r,:]. Query t=Vx modQ, c=Ut modQ and center each residue into [-Q/2,Q/2). Since abs(Wx)<=B<Q/2, the lift uniquely equals the full signed integer Wx. Python and separate C candidate read only stored factors/current x, not W. All factor coefficient slots are consumed; original information moved into the factors rather than vanished.

## Proof of the scoped minimal channel count
Over R=Z/(2^K), this r_K is the minimum inner dimension in W=UV. The displayed factors prove the upper bound. If W=UV with r columns in U, image(W) is a subgroup of image(U). The latter is a finite abelian2-group generated by at most r elements; its order2 torsion has F2 dimension<=r, and so does any subgroup's torsion. Each nonzero Smith cyclic summand contributes one independent order2 element to image(W). Thus r_K<=r. This is NOT ordinary real rank or a lower bound on all algorithms/work/reads.
