# VORTEX Research

VORTEX studies runtime-only execution of unmodified dense Transformer
checkpoints under an extreme hot-memory and latency contract.

## Language

**Fixed Mission**:
The unchanged E7 acceptance target in `AGENTS.md`: a real dense 405B-class
checkpoint, at most 8 GiB peak GPU VRAM, preserved output contract, and native
4B-Q4-class same-machine latency.
_Avoid_: Scaled-down goal, surrogate success

**Core Candidate**:
A causal executor mechanism with a fully charged credible route toward the
Fixed Mission, not merely a storage format, verifier, or isolated optimization.
_Avoid_: Interesting trick, partial solution

**Gate**:
A preregistered cheapest decisive test with fixed promotion and rejection
conditions that limits what may be implemented next.
_Avoid_: Benchmark sweep, exploratory tuning

**Surviving Candidate**:
A Core Candidate that has passed every Gate reached so far; it is not final
success and may still fail at the next evidence level.
_Avoid_: Solved, proven feasible

**Certain Research Milestone**:
The first positive milestone requested by the current research effort: a new
Core Candidate passes E0 resource closure and E1 real-weight falsification,
then demonstrates actual fail-closed dense-operation replacement at E2 on the
pinned small checkpoint.
_Avoid_: Another rejected family, final success

**Final Success**:
E7 evidence satisfying the Fixed Mission with independent reproduction.
_Avoid_: Projection, small-model success, estimated 405B result

**Cold-Backed Executor**:
An executor whose hot state may query the original checkpoint or another
lossless cold representation and charges every such query.
_Avoid_: Self-contained hot artifact, free oracle

**Synthetic Intermediate**:
An exact coefficient vector or linear form introduced by an automatic compiler
even though it is not an original matrix row or column.
_Avoid_: Terminal row, approximate latent

**Query-Adaptive Cold Source**:
A causal rule that uses the current committed activation and unchanged
checkpoint to choose a query-dependent subset of lossless cold information,
with selection, probes, misses, verification, and fallback fully charged.
_Avoid_: Static circuit stored on disk, free page oracle
