# VORTEX Research Efficiency Contract

Version: CTC-2026-09-05.

[Constructive Theory Contract](CONSTRUCTIVE_THEORY_CONTRACT.md) is authoritative for priorities and completion. Optimize closure of the fixed mission's essential proof/construction obligations, not experiment count or completeness of a mathematical taxonomy. Previous versions and their scope-specific failures remain in the policy history and scientific ledgers.

## Admission and concentration

Write the final theorem and O1-O6 ledger before selecting work. Compare three materially different premise-reversing principles with a concrete execution outline, exactness equation, fully charged resource equation, explicit >=10x elimination/amortization route, scale/lifting rationale, strongest counterexample and cheapest decisive Gate.

The >=10x route is an admission condition, not proof of the final p50/p95 target. The 4/405 ratio and approximately 1.185% heuristic are not universal runtime thresholds. Do not force A, N/A or r onto unrelated architectures. All model/hardware assumptions remain explicit.

Select the strongest credible survivor and work on its missing constructor, causal information source, exact native/state mapping or total-cost bound. Revisit alternatives each core round, but do not churn names or abandon a sound unfinished construction merely to generate another trio. An undeveloped idea may remain discovery work; it is not entitled to a large backend implementation.

## Cheapest decisive test, then construction

Use the cheapest applicable bound, exact counterexample/certificate, diagnostic favorable oracle, available real-checkpoint falsification or minimal reference before expensive backend/kernel work. A candidate's decisive failure ends that candidate immediately, not the whole research session. Continue available work on another credible principle or another essential obligation.

Before a primary synthetic study, state its exact target operation/obligation, native numerical and state lift, and scaling argument. A small theorem without this connection is auxiliary. A pass does not authorize endless larger toy instances or a full runtime; first close the missing target connection and paid costs. Timeouts/missing infrastructure are not impossibility evidence.

## Rejection and reopening

Reject/classify as auxiliary when the optimistic ceiling is merely incremental, overhead erases gain, arbitrary-model automation is absent, future target data or unbounded/free work is required, or the proposal only varies a rejected family's rank, seed, threshold, tile, block length or name.

Reopening needs a new information source, dependency, asymptotic mechanism or measured fact that invalidates the recorded rejection premise. Preserve exact failure scopes: a scoped no-go is neither universal impossibility nor proof that another candidate succeeds.

## Effort and progress

Default priorities: approximately 70% high-upside constructive execution theory, 20% cheapest decisive falsification, 10% auxiliary engineering. Do not fabricate time measurements. Small lemmas, negative tests, governance, test counts and commits must not displace the primary construction.

Record which essential obligation a task resolved, which assumption was eliminated/proved, how the full upper bound changed, and what decisive gap remains. Successful bookkeeping and family closure are not automatically increased feasibility. Use the three independent theory/hardware/handoff statuses; report method and proof before administrative metrics.
