# Find a Post-Atlas Causal Information Source

Type: task
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none

## Question

After EXP-083B rejected the common-spectral one-page Causal Residual Atlas,
does any materially different causal, checkpoint-derived, lossless information
source determine enough of the unread contribution to close the complete E0
operation, traffic, state, verification, miss, fallback, and compile equation
without training, checkpoint modification, target-future leakage, added
hardware, or a hidden dense discovery pass?

Nearby Atlas rank/page/layer/selector/bound variants are out of scope. A
candidate must state what new information exists, how it is constructed before
the query, why it is not another static circuit/replay/free proof trace, and
its cheapest decisive falsification.

## Answer

The strongest distinct source is decision-directional: cache exact primal and
dual images and ask only for `v^T W x`. Its exact decomposition exposes a
Bilinear Cross Residual `r^T W u` that neither cache determines. A rank-one
operator perturbation preserves both caches while changing this term.

Both concrete exact constructors fail E0. One fresh full-model dynamic dual
direction costs `1.5625%` over the registered 64-token lifetime, above the
complete `1.185185185%` target with all other costs free. A static one-last-
down vocabulary composite is `12.720703125 GiB` under a favorable two-byte
grant and costs `6.765979992%` to scan. The zero-forward EXP-083B diagnostic
also leaves every one of 248,319 competitors unresolved under the obvious
few-directions plus row-norm screen.

Decision: reject forward-only continuation as Atlas normal form, reject the
dynamic-raw and static-full-scan dual constructors, and retain only the
unsupported possibility of a different lossless sub-dense cross-residual
source. No experiment number is assigned. Evidence:
`../../../docs/research/E0_POST_ATLAS_CAUSAL_INFORMATION_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`.
