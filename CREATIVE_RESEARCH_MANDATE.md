# VORTEX Creative Research Mandate

Version: CTC-2026-09-05.

Read [Constructive Theory Contract](docs/CONSTRUCTIVE_THEORY_CONTRACT.md).

Before a core direction is selected, invent and compare three materially different execution principles. Reverse at least one hidden premise, computation order, information flow or verification unit. Do not begin with mere combinations/minor variants of familiar techniques or renamed failed families.

For each principle provide a concrete finite algorithm outline, original-output/state preservation equation, fully charged resource equation, explicit >=10x elimination/amortization route toward the final target, scale/native-lift rationale, strongest counterexample and cheapest decisive test.

Then concentrate on the strongest credible survivor and construct its missing causal, numerical, state and cost procedures. Each core continuation revisits the three-way comparison with current evidence, but need not invent new names or abandon a viable unfinished construction. A different direction requires genuinely different alternatives.

Fix the goal, not the architecture: block-verification A, N/A and r conditions are method-specific. Use established sound tools within a genuinely different principle; do not claim unprecedented novelty without evidence. A conditional existence statement is not the required complete constructive theory.

A rejected candidate ends promptly; other available high-value construction continues. A toy theorem, solver timeout, test pass or commit is not the goal. Governance-only work is not a core research round and must not invent experimental achievements.
