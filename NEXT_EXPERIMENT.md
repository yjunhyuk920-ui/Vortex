# Next constructive Gate

Date: 2026-09-05. Read the
[current decision/handoff](docs/research/E0_GLOBAL_DECODER_KERNEL_AUDIT.md) first.
No new numbered core experiment is authorized by the auxiliary 2x2 theorem.

## Required object

`NON_ENTRYWISE_REDUNDANT_GLOBAL_CODE_WITH_NATIVE_SUCCESSOR_STATE_AND_PAID_COSTS`

The goal remains exact, unmodified dense 405B on one 8-GiB GPU at the declared
same-machine native-4B-Q4 p50/p95. A parity decoder is not the goal.

A candidate must provide executable `encode`, causal physical-address selection,
`decode`, and a nontrivial exact or proven-bisimilar successor-state transition.
The SAME non-entrywise grammar must work at two sizes without separate exhaustive
truth tables. Explicitly charge original/lossless cold data, hot advice, words,
pages, address work, construction, decoding, activation/KV/work state,
verification, repair, fallback and wall time where measurable.

Before opening the next core round, compare three materially different principles
and establish a credible >=10x reduction with a route to final acceptance. Reject
an unimplemented source rather than supplying it as an oracle. Only an actual
survivor may advance to one complete public-checkpoint Transformer layer and
consecutive native token/successor-state validation.

## What this round settled and did not settle

The unrestricted nonlinear four-bit/two-probe 2x2 interface is impossible; the
five-bit positive interface is exact. This does not settle the 2x3/seven-bit
problem, the 25x108/50-word problem, native numerical lifting or VORTEX latency.
The seven-bit bounded synthesis returned UNKNOWN, which is not rejection.

Solving the seven-bit seed alone will NOT authorize a runtime backend. The
native lift, non-entrywise scaling and complete costs remain independent gates.
Do not spend the primary track on additional tiny zero-redundancy obstructions,
Feistel rank/seed sweeps, longer SMT timeouts, proof packaging without a causal
source, or dense prefix replay labeled a compressed state.

## Required evidence for promotion

A concrete constructor and decoder; independent exact controls and adversarial
cases; no future information; explicit native reduction ABI and successor-state
relation; deterministic reproduction; physical resource ledger; focused tests;
then actual operation replacement on an available unmodified public checkpoint.
Final 405B / 8 GiB / native-4B latency remains NOT TESTED until measured directly.

Use local validation then a new research-branch commit and remote SHA read-back.
Actions reruns are not required unless explicitly requested. Hardware service
changes, target downloads and benchmark allocation require their existing
separate authorization; this Gate grants none.
